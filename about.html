Our Story

Our Story

Three decades pursuing a single uncompromising standard.

Founded in the heart of Italy's industrial valley, PITEX began as a small precision tooling workshop with a single conviction: that olive oil production deserves the same engineering rigor as any high-performance machine. That conviction has since carried us into 42 countries, working alongside the world's most demanding producers.

Today, we sit at the intersection of traditional craftsmanship and Industry 4.0 — designing, manufacturing and commissioning turnkey extraction systems engineered for maximum yield, uncompromising quality, and decades of reliable operation.
